Cultural Guide

Bridge the urban-rural divide with respect and understanding

How to Greet

A simple "Namaskara" with folded hands goes a long way. It shows respect and instantly builds a connection with the local elders.

What to Wear

Modest, comfortable cotton clothing is best. Avoid overly revealing clothes to respect local customs and for comfort during activities.

Dining Etiquette

Food is often served on banana leaves. It's customary to eat with your right hand. Wait for the host to initiate the meal.
